# Residential Lease - Unit 4B
Maple Court Apartments, 120 Maple St, Cambridge, MA
Tenant: Kenji Watanabe  |  Landlord: Maple Court Properties LLC  |  Lease type: standard

## 1. Rent
1.1 Rent is due on the 1st of each month. A late fee of $50 applies after the 5th.

## 3. Access
3.1 Landlord will give 24 hours notice before entering, except in an emergency. This includes showings to prospective tenants or buyers during the last 60 days of the lease, with at least 24 hours notice.

## 7. Maintenance and repairs
7.1 Plumbing. Landlord maintains all plumbing, including pipes, drains, fixtures, and the water heater, and shall provide hot and cold running water at all times. Tenant pays for repairs of clogs or damage caused by misuse (for example flushing objects, grease, or wipes).
7.2 Heating and cooling. Landlord maintains heating and cooling systems. From September 15 to June 15, Landlord shall keep habitable rooms heated to at least 68F during the day and 64F at night.
7.3 Electrical. Landlord maintains wiring, outlets, switches, and breakers. Tenant replaces light bulbs.
7.4 Appliances. Landlord shall repair or replace appliances provided with the unit (refrigerator, stove, dishwasher) when they fail through normal use.
7.5 Locks and keys. Landlord repairs broken locks and entry doors. If Tenant is locked out due to lost or locked-in keys, Tenant pays a $75 lockout fee.
7.6 Pests. Landlord provides extermination for insects and rodents unless the infestation was caused by Tenant.
7.7 General. Landlord maintains walls, ceilings, floors, windows, doors, and cabinets in good repair. Cosmetic issues are handled within 14 days.

## 8. Tenant damage
8.1 Tenant pays for repair of any damage caused by Tenant, household members, or guests beyond normal wear and tear.

## 9. Emergencies
9.1 Emergencies include: no heat during heating season, no water or no hot water, flooding or active leaks, sewage backup, gas smell, electrical hazards (sparks, burning smell), loss of power to the unit, and lockouts or entry doors that will not lock. Landlord will respond within 24 hours.
9.2 Gas smell: Tenant must leave the unit immediately and call 911 or the gas utility before contacting the Landlord.
